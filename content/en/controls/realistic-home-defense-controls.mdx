Need to reload under pressure or find the lean button? The official *REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE* description lists controls for nine actions on PC and console. Mobile uses on-screen buttons that you can edit in Settings.

## PC and console controls

The [official REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE game description](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=10769857262) lists these bindings:

| Action | PC | Console |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Left Click | RT |
| Aim | Right Click | LT |
| Reload | R | X |
| Change fire mode | V | RB |
| Sprint | Shift | L3 |
| Crouch / prone | C | B |
| Lean | Q or E | D-Pad |
| Switch weapon | 1–9 | Y or LB |
| Interact | G | X |

## Buttons worth noting

The console list assigns **X** to both reload and interact. The description does not explain how the game chooses between those actions, so check the on-screen prompt when you need to interact.

It also groups crouch and prone under one button—**C** on PC or **B** on console—without specifying how to switch between the two positions.

## Mobile controls

On mobile, *REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE* uses on-screen buttons. The official description says they are editable in **Settings**, but it does not provide a fixed mobile button layout or describe the available customization options.

## FAQ

### What are the REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE controls for firing and aiming?

On PC, use Left Click to fire and Right Click to aim. On console, use RT to fire and LT to aim.

### How do I reload?

Press **R** on PC or **X** on console.

### How do I lean?

Use **Q** or **E** on PC, or the **D-Pad** on console.

### Can I change the mobile controls?

The official description says mobile’s on-screen buttons are editable in **Settings**.
